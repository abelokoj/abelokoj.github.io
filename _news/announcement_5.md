On 10 April 2026, at the department's annual Math Honors Day, I was awarded the Dwight B. Goodner Mathematics Fellowship by the Department of Mathematics at Florida State University. This is the senior award, given for outstanding teaching in mathematics by a graduate teaching assistant. It follows the department's Distinguished Graduate Teaching Assistant Award, which I received at Math Honors Day in April 2025.

Over the year the fellowship recognizes, I taught MAC 2313 Calculus with Analytic Geometry III in Spring 2025, Fall 2025 and Spring 2026, and MAS 3105 Applied Linear Algebra I in Summer 2025. Since Fall 2023 I have taught as instructor of record in seven consecutive terms. The details of each course are on my [teaching page]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}). I am grateful to my students, to the faculty who observed and mentored my teaching, and to the department for the recognition.

### About the fellowship

The fellowship comes from an endowed fund established by Millie and Dwight Goodner to recognize teaching excellence in mathematics at Florida State, and it is awarded to graduate teaching assistants in the Department of Mathematics. It is one of the department's named awards for teaching, together with the Distinguished Graduate Teaching Assistant Award, the Tam Family Award for Excellence in Graduate Student Teaching and the Bettina Zoeller Richmond Award, all presented each spring at Math Honors Day.
