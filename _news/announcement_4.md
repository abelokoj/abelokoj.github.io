On 11 April 2025, at the department's annual Math Honors Day in the Love Building, I received the Distinguished Graduate Teaching Assistant Award from the Department of Mathematics at Florida State University. The award recognizes graduate teaching assistants whose classroom teaching the department considers exemplary.

By then I had taught as an instructor of record for three semesters, with full responsibility for my own sections: MAC 1140 Precalculus Algebra (Fall 2023) and MAC 2311 Calculus with Analytic Geometry I (Spring and Fall 2024). In Spring 2025 I was teaching MAC 2313 Calculus with Analytic Geometry III. The details of each course are on my [teaching page]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}). Before that, I had worked in the department's labs and recitations, first as a proctor and then as a recitation leader.

### About the award

The Distinguished Graduate Teaching Assistant Award is one of several awards the department gives to its graduate teaching assistants for excellence in teaching. The others are the Goodner Teaching Award, the Tam Family Award for Excellence in Graduate Student Teaching and the Bettina Zoeller Richmond Award. Teaching assistants earn classroom responsibility in stages. They first proctor and assist experienced lecturers, then complete a semester-long college teaching internship. Next they teach a section of their own under faculty and peer mentors, and only after becoming doctoral candidates can they teach the calculus sequence. The awards are presented each spring at Math Honors Day, where faculty, students and staff gather to recognize the department's achievements over the year.
