and assessment feedback. A coordinated multi-section course following a common
departmental syllabus and shared assessments.

## Course Overview

An applied introduction to linear algebra for students in mathematics, engineering, and the sciences, developing the theory of linear systems alongside the matrix factorizations that make them computationally useful.

## Topics

- Systems of linear equations, row reduction, and vector and matrix equations
- Matrix algebra, inverses, and LU factorization
- Determinants
- Vector spaces, subspaces, basis, and dimension
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, and diagonalization
- Orthogonality, projections, least-squares problems, and QR factorization
- Symmetric matrices, quadratic forms, and the singular value decomposition
